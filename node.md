## LARGEST SAFE STEP

Stage 2 (self-poke) does NOT have a target family yet under the pre-registered rule -- but it now has one strong lead. The layer-0 units-digit family T5_10 (212/232 in layer 0) mean-ablated cuts the gold sum's log-prob by 0.686 nats and exact match 0.975 -> 0.775, where 4 of 5 layer-matched random sets of the same 232 do nothing (|drop| <= 0.013, exact 0.97-0.975). The fifth random set (s0) destroys addition outright (drop 3.67, exact 0.155): some layer-0 neuron(s) in it are catastrophic to ablate, independent of periodicity, and it alone sinks D2. The next rung (one CPU round, same checkpoint, same saved activations, no new model work besides ablation): (a) find s0's critical neuron(s) by bisecting the s0 set (log2 212 ~ 8 forwards), and (b) re-run D2 for T5_10 against >= 20 layer-matched random sets that EXCLUDE pre-registered high-impact layer-0 neurons identified on a held-out problem set, scoring rank among randoms (p-value) rather than a 5-set max. T2 should be dropped as a stage-2 target: ablating it slightly HELPS (-0.012). Do not poke until T5_10 beats a fair random band.

## Caveats

- D1 misses by 32 neurons (0.473 vs 0.5 pct); the twin null got STRICTER after detrending (0.6564 vs raw 0.6069), so this is not a detrending artefact helping the null. One twin seed only.
- 237 of 552 D1 members are still T100 after detrend: curved (non-linear) monotone codes leave k=1 power; they count toward D1 but belong to no family.
- D2's max-of-5 rule is sensitive to a single outlier random draw: random s0's 3.67 drop compares with |drop| <= 0.013 for s1-s4; its cause is not identified here (the top-magnitude last-token neurons in s0 are not exceptional: L0.70 mean |act| 0.59 vs layer-0 q999 0.70). A post-hoc reading only; the verdict stands as pre-registered.
- T5_10 is a layer-0 family: ablating it likely removes digit-token read-in rather than a mid-network addition circuit; the layer-matched control isolates periodicity from layer, not from 'reads the digit token'.
- Mean-ablation uses the last-token mean over the value prompts, applied at every position (including non-'+' positions where the neuron's natural mean differs); recorded in params.json `mean_def`.
- Exact match baseline 0.975 is the same ceiling as run 1; the log-prob score resolves sub-flip damage (T2 random spread +-0.014).
- Instruct checkpoint (run 1 comparability), raw completion, fp32 CPU; saved activations are float16 storage of the float32 values the analysis used.
- C3 reported only: overlap 2 vs 4.71 expected (layer-stratified, number tokens masked) -- the per-turn position bridge is not supported.
