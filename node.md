## RULER

For a fixed CPU-second budget there exists a cooperation scheme (sampling + verifier, routing, cascade, speculative, shared cache, debate, coupled byte-neuron channels, ...) whose accuracy exceeds the best single member at that budget, and whose marginal gain per added member or sample does not decay — an accelerating segment of the accuracy-vs-compute curve with a bootstrap CI over items that excludes zero. Baseline is always the best member (or one model at matched compute), never a sum. Measured in the Vision-3 Pareto table (CP9).
