THE PRIME OVERRULED MY DESIGN AND WAS RIGHT, and the correction is worth more than the round. I had three stall shapes and was about to hand out a third detector -- I had even told the helper to look for a shared primitive, which was the right instinct at the wrong altitude. The prime's reading: all three are one root wearing three faces, NO AUTHORITY EVER RECONCILES A RECORD AGAINST REALITY, and a detector per shape is N detectors with the fourth already waiting. One reconciler turns all three into queries over a corpus that stopped lying. I could see the pattern and not the level; that is the difference a second reader makes and it belongs in the record rather than in a thank you.

THE ONE-WAY RULE IS THE WHOLE SAFETY ARGUMENT and it is stated three times in the claim because it is the sentence a kid will paraphrase away. A dead pid PROVES the process stopped. A live pid proves NOTHING -- pids are reused. Derive terminal from death only; never derive `running` from life. The failure mode of getting this wrong is a corpse resurrected under a recycled pid: silent, unreproducible, and the kind this project has spent all day learning to hate. So falsifiers (c) and (d) both assert the non-action on a live pid, from opposite sides.

THE FIXTURE IS FROZEN AND REAL, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. `.agi/worktrees/a00-e9572046/.agi/sessions/iter-L4.85/` still holds the kid's `agent.json` stuck at `status: running` with a pid I watched die, and the parent's manifest beside it. Today's lesson, four times over, was that a fixture more generous than reality certifies nothing -- so the brief forbids inventing one and forbids touching that worktree. It is the only capture of this shape anyone has.

WHAT I DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT: the parent's poll-loop timeout. It is a real belt -- a kid can be ALIVE and WEDGED, which no reconciler will ever catch -- and the prime ruled it second precisely so it cannot become the fix. A timeout added now would make the round green while leaving the corpus still lying, and the next shape would arrive with nothing to catch it.
