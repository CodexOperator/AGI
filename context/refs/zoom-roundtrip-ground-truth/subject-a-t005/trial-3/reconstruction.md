## Deterministic Procedural Dungeon Generator

Build a complete seeded dungeon generation pipeline that produces reproducible spatial layouts and Three.js renderings from a single integer seed. The system chains five components in order: (1) a seeded PRNG module providing deterministic randomness, (2) a grid-based room layout generator with organic variation, (3) a corridor connectivity algorithm linking rooms via pathfinding, (4) a Three.js mesh renderer converting the topology into floor and wall geometry, and (5) a verification suite confirming bit-for-bit reproducibility. All randomness must flow through the seeded RNG to guarantee that identical seeds produce identical dungeons, allowing players to respawn in the same space or share seed values for consistent multiplayer experiences.
