# doc:l4-formation-1-prime-only

**Formation 1 of 4 under goal:g15.** The Prime alone, no director, no Keep, no Masters. It is the formation season 1 ran on, and the one every later formation exists to stop repeating.

The order that names the four formations and this one first (the only owner line that speaks of F1 by name):

> Then under that town you have different formations. First it’s just Prime only. Then it’s the Texas two-step, then hybrid with gradual expansion. Then full activation but we aren’t ready for that yet. Each one helps bootstrap the next gradually so no one agent has to take on too much too suddenly, other than prime, who had the whole weight on his shoulders in season 1.

Every earlier owner line in the doc (from 2026-09-09) already has one director beside the Prime, so the Prime-only formation predates the doc's record; season 1 is the graph's first season, closed at the first rollover (the `season 1 vision, closed at the first rollover` line on every season-1 vision node). Nothing else is asserted about it here.

## The formation

```
owner ──► belam (Prime) ── every role at once: the graph's trajectory · goals · briefs ·
             │             pi dispatch · review · merge · commit · push · handoff
             └── pi parents ──► kids            (paid workers per round; no persistent seat below the Prime)
   no director · no Keep (SM / Sensei) · no town masters · no Council
```

**Prime/SM reading:** one persistent seat. The Prime is Council, Keep, Masters and director in one context window — the load the owner names as "the whole weight" (L775). The seam out is survival mode (L30, L265): Prime + ONE director-kid on a loop brief, two persistent seats "each helping the other to conserve the resource that matters most". That single director is the first seat the Prime hands weight to.

## What it bootstraps in the next

Next: **doc:l4-formation-2-texas-two-step** (goal:g17.1).

**Prime/SM reading:** F1 proves the loop itself — one seat can run brief → dispatch → review → merge → rotate end to end — and establishes the Prime's successor chain (`belam-S<season>-L<loop>-<numeral>`, derived from the live ladder cells) and the handoff/card discipline the later seats copy. Survival mode, the step at F1's edge, proves that a second persistent seat can be trusted with a loop brief without cratering the subscription (L265, L387: "That seemed to work phenomenally last time"). F2 then doubles that one director:

> Let's gently dial up the concurrency: spawn a second director kid and let them collab together to split the work. Act after the workflow finishes. Let the current director take point though and the other one is like a helper to the first. Call it the Texas two step formation, goes under sanctuary goal as subgoal

(goal:g17.1 L26)

> They're like the Council, the Keep, and the Masters all rolled into one role since this is survival mode

(goal:g17.1 L28)

**Prime/SM reading:** what F1 leaves the two-step is the thing it could not do itself — a director who has already carried a brief alone, so that splitting one brief across point + helper is a small step, not a new trust. What it never bootstraps is the Prime's own relief: even in F3 the owner keeps "Prime resides over core town himself directly during hybrid survival mode." (doc:l4-owner-decisions L773) — the Prime's weight is shed only in F4.
