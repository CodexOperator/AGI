# experiment:a00-5e117964

## Experiment

Test the parent hypothesis by inspecting the cold director-brief artifact that
is actually injected into seats. The acceptance surface is the unified brief's
`golden` route line, not the parent goal table alone: it must expose the
pane-facing contract names `write`, `read`, `send`, `dispatch/workflow`, and
`rotate/spawn` without inventing a sixth route.

## Evidence

The cold brief artifact `.agi/nodes/doc/unified-director-brief.md` contains
this compact route contract in its standing LLM ops section:

```text
routes write·read·send·dispatch/workflow·rotate/spawn
```

The same artifact's diagram and workflow rules consistently map those names
to `write.py`, `send.py`, `dispatch.py`/`workflow.py`, and `rotate.py`; no
separate grok-only route is named. The experiment therefore supports the
hypothesis that the cold seat brief lists the five routes by contract name.

## Falsifier

A fresh cold-brief render that omits any one of the five names, or adds a
sixth special grok route, would disprove this claim. This experiment did not
execute an adapter-specific injection; it verified the durable brief artifact
that the adapter's role/customization path consumes.
