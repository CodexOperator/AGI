## Implication for goal:g14.15.2 (swarm telepathy)

Every shift-capable family here is the lone `qwen3` model (Bonsai 1.7B's base): of the four
runnable GGUFs, three are `qwen35`/`qwen35moe` and `get_can_shift()` returns **false**, so a
KV-span-exchanging swarm cannot include them under this pinned llama.cpp. Any swarm built on
Bonsai 2 27B / Qwen3.5-9B / Qwen3.5-35B-A3B therefore needs the saved-TEXT-plus-tail-KV
fallback, or a llama.cpp change that makes shift legal for IMROPE (a per-position RoPE
concern, not merely re-indexing).
