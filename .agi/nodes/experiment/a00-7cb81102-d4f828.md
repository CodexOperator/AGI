# experiment:a00-7cb81102-d4f828

**TEL.02: what `--cache-reuse` can and cannot do on this rig.** The hypothesis
needs an ARBITRARY-POSITION KV shift of a captured span. The rig gives
SAME-PREFIX reuse (100 pct token agreement, ~0.09 of prefill tokens) and gives
NO shift at all — `--cache-reuse 8` is in the router argv, is forwarded to the
9B child, and is disabled at load by the context itself. The mechanism is
ARCHITECTURALLY UNAVAILABLE for `qwen35`, not merely unconfigured.

## 7. Plain statement of the distinction

`--cache-reuse` provides **SAME-PREFIX reuse** — the new prompt is an extension
(or repetition) of the cached prefix, so the KV needs no new positions. The
hypothesis needs an **ARBITRARY-POSITION shift** of a captured span `[a:b]` to a
later position p'. These are NOT the same claim. Here even the same-prefix
machinery is switched off by the context, and the arbitrary-position shift is
**ARCHITECTURALLY UNAVAILABLE** on `qwen35` (IMROPE, `n_pos_per_embd()==4`,
`get_can_shift()==false`, `seq_add` guarded by a hard assert). Same-position
fidelity is therefore NOT evidence for the hypothesis; it is a measurement of a
different, weaker mechanism that the rig happens to support.
