# experiment:a00-81747954-messaging-r2

Second cut under `goal:g7.32.2`, building on `experiment:a00-885927de-messaging-r1`.
Closes r1's two recorded defects: the whitespace-only harness that silently
returned `"native"`, and the cross-harness "trace" whose transport was a stub,
never the real `send.py`.

### Change 1 — whitespace-only harness is UNKNOWN

`extensions/agi/bin/messaging.py` now normalizes string harnesses with a
private `_identity()`. `route("  ", "  ")` raises `UnknownHarness`; a padded
`" grok-bot "` routes by its stripped identity (native against `"grok-bot"`).
Non-strings pass through unchanged. `rotate`/`dispatch` still not imported.

### Change 2 — the trace invokes the REAL send.py

No edit to `send.py` (out of scope, `goal:g7.32.4`). The transport seam is
bound to the real module: `send.send(root, to, text, sender, nudge=False)`.
The nudge seam writes a real artifact file. One call records
`[nudge.write, send.send]` and the inbox block lands on disk.
