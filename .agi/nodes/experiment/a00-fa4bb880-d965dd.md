energy mask is a stricter K-cache reduction for the same q-side budget.

## Why the profile over-promises (for hop 3)

The hop-1 energy is the **variance** of `c_p = q_p k_p + q_{p+32} k_{p+32}` over
causal position pairs, normalised per head. Variance ignores the **mean**: a pair
with a small variance but a systematically large mean contribution is ranked
"low energy" yet removing it shifts attention logits by a near-constant bias, which
re-weights attention everywhere. The profile is also prompt-averaged and strongly
correlated across the 7 query heads sharing a KV head, so per-head pruning compounds
inside a group rather than averaging out. A mean-aware or logit-perturbation-based
energy (e.g. variance + squared mean, or measured sensitivity of the layer output)
is the natural next candidate; the K-side union table above is what hop 3 needs.
