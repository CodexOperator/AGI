## Target end-state
- A post is moved to the other hub ONLY on a recorded quota limit: the state file's `quota` line for that post's (hub, model), written from the hub's own cooldown. Never on hub-down alone, never on an unread or stale state, never on a post's symptom (mail, a stalled screen).
- The target hub is checked first: the move happens only if the OTHER hub's state line for that model reads ok and is fresh. A move into a hub that is itself in quota, down or unread does not happen, and the refusal is said once.
- The move is LOUD (sp): ONE line to the said file and the alarm spool names the post, the model, the hub it left, the hub it went to, the quota reset time, and the reason `quota`. A move with no such line is a defect.
- The move is bounded: at most one move per post per cooldown. NO automatic move back is built (the owner was not asked): a post stays where it was moved until the owner or belam moves it, and the said file carries ONE standing line per moved post (`post X on hub B since HH:MMZ, moved from A on quota`) so nobody forgets it moved. The move-back and which account a crew post may move onto are BANKED for the owner through belam.
- The mover is its own unit. stall_watch.sh and hub_quota.py `say` still act on nothing and move nothing; their old no-move lines are updated in this leaf's tip to point here (see Invariants).
- Scope is exactly Q3's text: any post, the other hub, quota only. A wider scope (hub-down moves, a move back, an account rule) is a question to the owner, not a build.

- NEVER on hub-down alone. The 11-10 night (14:22Z-19:34Z, both hubs unable to resolve upstream) is a day on which a move would have helped nobody and spent the other hub's quota.
