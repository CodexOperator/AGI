## Why this exists
goal:g7.16.1.11: a pane's mail wake is a background shell loop that ends for good when its `sleep` is killed or fails, and nothing restarts it. belam measured it (belam [red] 19:1xZ, SM placement 19:1xZ): DG1, DG2, DG5, all-is-one and self-perpetuating had all lost the box-wake loop, and DG1 sat on 8 unread box messages from 04:51Z to 19:0xZ with no wake. Bytes at trunk 3b733afbb2, engine-wrap.md `### agi-run` (773 B): :24 the pane-cap loop `(while sleep 300;do ...;done)&`, :25 the send.py inbox loop `(... while sleep 5;do ...;done)&`, :26 the box-wake loop `(s=0;while sleep 5;do n=$(AGI_POST=... box n ...);[ $n -gt $s ]&&printf "mail: box read">$i ...;done)&`, then :27 `exec strace ... $H $c go`. In sh `while sleep N` tests the exit status of `sleep`, so a killed or failed sleep (a pkill, an OOM kill of the sleep, a signal to the process group) leaves the loop for good; and because agi-run `exec`s into strace at :27, no shell is left to supervise the loops. The pi route (cccc.ts, engine-wrap.md:42-43) is a node interval with `.unref()`, not a `while sleep` loop, and is out of this leaf.
