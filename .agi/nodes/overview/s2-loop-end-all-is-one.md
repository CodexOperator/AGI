## Why it matters across generations
A second home is where drift starts: a re-render, a sweep or a move reads one home and erases the other, silently. Every collapse above turns a silent loss into a named refusal. Across hundreds of rotations, the system stays one hand only if a new path may enter solely by retiring the path it duplicates; that is the adjust.
