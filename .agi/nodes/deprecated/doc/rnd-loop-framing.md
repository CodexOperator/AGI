## FRAME — before every mint, and again after every disproof or surprise
| ask | what it catches |
|---|---|
| restate the goal in ONE line, your own words | a goal you cannot restate is not framed yet |
| **is there a BIGGER picture going on here?** — what is this a special case of · what would make it unnecessary · who already solved the general form | the lever that sits one level up |
| **is there a SMALLER, SIMPLER one?** — the cheapest test that could disprove it · the one-variable version · the baseline nobody ran | a round that is larger than its question |
| what result would change your mind? | a claim with no falsifier (not a hypothesis yet) |
| what exists already? — the graph (same claim, prior verdicts) · the treasury · papers | spend on a question already answered |

Pick the frame that yields a falsifiable test inside the ceiling; write it on the node in one line: `frame: bigger | as-given | smaller — <why>`.

## IDEA → HYPOTHESIS
```
idea        a direction ("X might move Y")                              → node type idea
hypothesis  CLAIM (with a number) · FALSIFIER · MEASURE (metric + baseline) · CHEAPEST TEST · CEILING · IF-WRONG (the next move)
round       one variable per round · a baseline beside every number · warm-up before any tok/s · replicate before you believe it
```

## After the verdict
```
proved        ─▶ push further: try the BIGGER frame
disproved     ─▶ WHY (agi-research-review mints the why idea) ─▶ agi-brainstorm (1-5 hypotheses, adversarial keep/modify/drop) ─▶ REFRAME
inconclusive  ─▶ the SMALLER frame: cut the test until it can answer
no signal, round after round on one line ─▶ stop the line, say so on the board, REFRAME (when to stop = the master's call)
```

## Nesting is not the R&D shape
An R&D chain grows idea → hypothesis → experiment → why → next hypothesis, not a sub-goal tree. Nest only when a goal genuinely splits into independent questions; never to fill slots.
