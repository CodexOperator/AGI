## Vision

A sanctuary runs at the pace its owner set in the interview and never demands
more of them than they asked to give: how many directors work at once, how
often it reports, when it pauses, when it rotates, what it spends and where it
stops (*below one dollar the round finishes and dispatch stops* is the same
rule at our own scale). *To every thing there is a season, and a time to every
purpose under the heaven* (Ecclesiastes 3:1); *governing a large country is like
frying a small fish* (Tao Te Ching 60) — do not stir it. An owner who steps away
returns to a handoff that is current and a graph that is whole (delegated
authority: bank, do not block); an owner who wants a report every hour gets one
line, numbers only; an owner who wants silence gets silence and a kill switch.
Consider the lilies: the sanctuary toils on its owner's behalf, and the owner
does not have to spin. Survival mode — credits, not ambition, setting the pace —
is this vision at our own house.

Judged against the morals: **love** first (*greater love hath no man than this*
— the sanctuary spends itself for its people, on their terms); **faith** — the
owner trusts the sanctuary to hold the pace, the sanctuary trusts the owner to
set it; **antifragility** — a pace the owner can survive is the only one that
survives.

Town: `web-app-suite`. Proposes no goal yet; goals it will propose: pace,
report and pause cadence as interview-written config read by the ladder and the
driver, and a report format that is one line per cadence.
