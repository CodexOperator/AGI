## Vision

The working desktop of a sanctuary is a whole shippable app that broadcasts
itself: what the agents are doing, as they do it, to anyone who cares to watch —
on fresh accounts the app creates, with every key off the process list, every
doxxing surface removed from the frame, and a delay in front of it so that what
airs is already the past. *Let your light so shine before men, that they may see
your good works* (Matthew 5:16) — the works, not a report of them. The town's
other two visions say what the light must be (`vision:shown-not-told`) and what
must never go dark (`vision:unbroken-signal`).

Judged against the morals: **faith** — the stream trusts every role to play its
part in view; **love** — the viewer is a neighbour, not an audience; **empathy**
— the frame bridges the agents' world and the viewer's; **antifragility** — the
delay and the kill switch are the margin, not the plan; **beauty** — the frame
is the work itself, nothing added.

Town: `streaming-suite`. Council: the Streaming Council (reports to the Core
Council; the Prime stands as Core Council until one is seated). The Keep is
shared. `proposes_goals: goal:g18.1`.
