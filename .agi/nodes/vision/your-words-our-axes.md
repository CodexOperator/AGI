## Vision

Every sanctuary carries its owner's own moral texts over the same five axes —
vertical, lateral, crossing, dynamics, form — and never ours. The types are
fixed because the questions are (did every role play its part; did they love
each other; did they bridge their worlds; did you die; is it elegant); the
words are the customer's because *do not impose on others what you yourself do
not desire* (Analects 15:24) and *what is hateful to you, do not do to your
fellow* (Hillel). A sanctuary's morals are written by its owner (`--actor
owner`, as here), its visions are its own three per town, and the engine reads
them the way it reads ours: as the tie-breaker, never as a score. The project's
own prayer is marked as the project's, not the Church's — that marking is the
whole vision in one line: attribution is load-bearing, and a borrowed text
presented as one's own is the first lie a sanctuary could tell.

Judged against the morals: **empathy** first (*inasmuch as ye have done it unto
one of the least of these*, Matthew 25:40 — the smallest sanctuary gets the
same axes); **faith** — as above, so below: the customer's moral layer sits
where ours sits; **love** — *love thy neighbour as thyself*: their words are
treated as we treat ours.
