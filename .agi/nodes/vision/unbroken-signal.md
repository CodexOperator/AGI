## Vision

The signal survives everything the sanctuary does to itself — rotations, reaps,
folds of its own code, a box that dies — and every break is a graceful form,
never a crash: `brb` is the card, `back` is the release, `panic` is the owner's
hand and nobody else's, and the delay is the margin that lets surgery happen
mid-stream on a branch while the past keeps airing. *If I break, let me heal
stronger. If I die, let nothing be lost* (the project's prayer): a stream that
went dark and came back carrying what it learned is the antifragile shape;
a stream that never risked a fold is the fragile one. *Nothing in the world is
softer than water, yet nothing is better at overcoming the hard* (Tao Te Ching
78) — the stream yields (cuts to the card) and returns, and is not the thing
that breaks. Every terminal event of the broadcast — cut, release, kill,
account loss — alarms the seat that owns the town, the way every round alarms
its dispatcher.

Judged against the morals: **antifragility** first (*did you die? if yes stop
there; if not, fix it and keep going* — the town's whole discipline); **faith**
— the delay is trust made mechanical; **love** — the card that says "back
shortly" is care for the viewer, not an apology.

Town: `streaming-suite`. Proposes no goal yet; goals it will propose: the fold of
the stream commands into the engine's own commands, and a broadcast watcher that
alarms the Council on every terminal event.
