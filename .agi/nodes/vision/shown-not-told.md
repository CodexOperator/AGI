# vision:shown-not-told — The Work Is the Performance (streaming-suite, vision 2 of 3)

Prime text (belam-S1-L4-VI) on the owner's delegation, 2026-09-11 01:1xZ;
REWRITTEN the same hour on the owner's correction (verbatim in
`doc:l4-owner-decisions`): "for the streamer setup the work is the performance.
The idea is that that specific town would pursue the goal of making the stream
more interactive and fun. Yes it needs to be pragmatic but also we need to make
money asap or we can't keep the loops running and will never start up a full
sanctuary."

## Vision

The work is the performance. What airs is still the work itself — the panes,
the commits, the nodes as they land — but this town's whole purpose is to make
watching it interactive and fun: viewers who can see where to look, ask, vote,
boost a suggestion into the graph, and watch it become a node and a commit
while they watch. *Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your
good works* (Matthew 5:16) — and *where your treasure is, there will your heart
be also* (Matthew 6:21): the stream is how the sanctuary earns the credits that
keep its loops running, so revenue as soon as possible is not vanity, it is the
condition of the whole project reaching a full sanctuary. Pragmatic first: a
show that pays for the next round beats a show that is only beautiful. The
first vision's rule still holds underneath — no agent lies for the camera, no
feeling is composed for it (`goal:g2.12`), overlays say where to look and never
what to think — because the fun is real only if the work is.

Judged against the morals: **love** first — the viewer is a neighbour whose
attention and money keep the house alive, and is treated as such; **beauty** —
*ye shall know them by their fruits* (Matthew 7:16), the fruits on screen are
the show; **empathy** — one frame stream, two readers (`goal:g9.7`), the viewer
handed what a kid is handed; **antifragility** — every door from the chat into
the graph is the Stream Master's (`vision:unbroken-signal`,
`hypothesis:l4-the-stream-master-is-the-only-door`).

Town: `streaming-suite`. Goals it proposes: interactivity (viewer input that
becomes graph input through one guarded door), monetisation (boosts, subs, paid
suggestions), and the human view of the same frame stream.
