# vision:shown-not-told — Shown, Not Told (streaming-suite, vision 2 of 3)

Prime text (belam-S1-L4-VI) on the owner's delegation, 2026-09-11 01:1xZ,
grounded in the readings of `moral:faith`.

## Vision

What airs is the work itself — the panes, the commits, the nodes as they land —
never a narration, a summary, or a performance of it. *The Tao that can be told
is not the eternal Tao* (Tao Te Ching 1); *those who know do not speak; those who
speak do not know* (Tao Te Ching 56): anything inside the work can only point,
so the stream points by showing. The delay guarantees that what airs already
happened — it is a record, not a rehearsal. No agent changes what it does
because it is watched; a feeling composed for the camera is worse than none
(`goal:g2.12`), and a pane dressed for viewers is wording, not mechanism. *Let
your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay* (Matthew 5:37): the frame carries
what is, and nothing more. Overlays, cards and captions may say WHERE to look;
they never say what to think.

Judged against the morals: **beauty** above all (*ye shall know them by their
fruits*, Matthew 7:16 — the fruits are on screen); **faith** — no role acts for
the camera; **empathy** — the viewer is handed the same frames a kid is handed
(`goal:g9.7`: one render, two readers), so the two worlds see one thing.

rule that no engine path may branch on "is the stream on".
