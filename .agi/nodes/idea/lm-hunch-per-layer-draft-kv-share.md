# idea:lm-hunch-per-layer-draft-kv-share

## Owner hunch (verbatim)
"use hierarchical decoding where each layer predicts 2-3 tokens or even kv-caches somehow of the layer below" (H2, 2026-09-16)

## What the sources say (measured lines with paths)
- papers/arxiv-2510-05421.md — the 1-level instance is measured: layer 2 drafts k_spec=4, layers 3-32 verify (greedy, lossless), MAT 3.04-3.61, 2.16x on Vicuna-7B/H100 (Table 2); Medusa parallel multi-token heads 1.66x, EAGLE-2 2.18x. Hit/miss-only training collapses: CE-only MAT 0.039 (0.335x), PG-only 0.035; KL to verifier logits first (1.933). Multi-level ladder untested. Tied head = 26.1% of Qwen3-0.6B bytes, 27.6% of Qwen2.5-0.5B; one layer ~2.6-3.0%.
- papers/baseten-eagle3-heads.md (critique 4,8,12) — "predict from the layer below" already ships as EAGLE-3: the yuyijiong/Qwen3.5-4B-Eagle3 head reads hidden states of layers [1,15,28]; 3 steps 2.76 accepted/1.91x, 7 -> 3.50/2.10x, 15 -> 3.66/1.70x (card, 1 GPU); blog: 3-4 draft tokens, "8 rarely helps"; llama.cpp 093a2f8 has --spec-type draft-eagle3.
- papers/osd-2310-07177.md — Eq. 2 E=(1-a^(k+1))/((1-a)(kc+1)); measured v4 (A100, k=8, c=0.13): 0.95x at a=0.5, 1.30x at 0.7, 2.55x at 0.9. a and c are the whole ledger.
- papers/deepseek-v4-1-flash.md L82-89, L423-427, L521-522 — the one trained "KV of the layer below": CED projects the top-20 layers' global KV from H_{L/2} via per-layer W^KV,W^Z ("nearly halves prefill", from scratch, no retrofit); DSpark 3-block drafter emits 5 positions per pass, no acceptance number. Qwen3-0.6B f16 KV 112 KB/token = ~470 MB read at depth 4096 vs 0.64 GB Q8 weights; q4_0 KV -> ~130 MB; attention ~0.94 vs weights 1.2 GFLOP/token [ESTIMATE].
- papers/glm-5-3-flash.md — MTP accept length 2.76 at 4 speculative steps, 3 MTP layers sharing parameters. papers/arxiv-2607-24653.md — K3 draft = MTP layer unrolled 7 steps on AttnRes block 1/4/final features, LK loss.
- papers/arxiv-2603-12201.md — adjacent-layer top-k overlap 0.7-1.0, yet similarity-DP 49.8 < uniform 50.7 < searched; which layers may share is found only by greedy LM-loss search.
- trove-survey-2026-09-14.md L525, L536-539, L573 — the hunch IS a looped-model property: Ouro last-step KV reuse = 4x KV reduction at <0.15% loss; adaptive exit averages 2.5 of 4 loops (~4x compute, 66% MMLU); 92% of Ouro-2.6B tokens match the final output by loop 3; H6/C5 (1-loop draft, 4-loop verify) seeded, unrun. ## Actionable claims
1. Verify is near-free up to ~4 tokens on this box: t(batch-4 forward)/t(1-token) <= 1.5 for Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 and <= 2.0 for Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M. FALSIFIER: >= 2x -> every hierarchical/multi-token scheme is capped < 2x here; >= 3x -> dead (also decides H7/C5/EAGLE-3 seeds at once). TEST: llama-bench -p 1,2,3,4,5,6,8 -n 0 -r 5 -o json, then -p 0 -n 32, both models, A1 log protocol. 5 min, $0.
2. No untrained layer of Qwen2.5-0.5B can draft: logit-lens top-1 (final norm + tied head on the layer-k residual) sits below the full-head break-even curve at every layer — a >= 0.7 for k <= 2, >= 0.8 for k <= 7, >= 0.9 for k <= 14 (1.2x target, k_spec 4, DVI block model with 3.0%/layer + 27.6% head; block = 0.0906k + 2.104 passes) — and agreement two tokens ahead is < 10% everywhere. FALSIFIER: any layer above the curve -> a free self-speculative split exists; run it before training anything. TEST: one output_hidden_states forward over the D1 tokens, 24 agreement numbers + Eq. 2 per layer (head matmul per layer per 512-token window). ~5-8 min CPU at the box's ~50 fp32 GFLOPS, $0.
3. Untrained adjacent-layer KV sharing on Qwen3-0.6B (layer l+1 attends over layer l's k_norm/v_proj outputs, modeling_qwen3.py L253-254, RoPE is position-only) costs > 0.05 nats for every one of the 27 pairs (D1 price: 0.176 per 3.2% of bytes; one pair's KV is 1.5% of the 1.11 GB/token at 4K). FALSIFIER: >= 7 pairs at <= 0.05 nats each, picked greedily by measured loss -> a free ~25%-KV cut (<= 1.12x at 4K on the ledger), worth an engine patch after the q4_0 sweep. TEST: hooks capture layer l, overwrite l+1, teacher-forced loss on the D1 tokens; 27 pairs x ~55 s = ~25 min CPU (13 odd pairs ~12 min; seconds each on local-town), $0; needs Qwen3-0.6B loaded fp32 (2.4 GB; bf16 matmul is 2 GFLOPS on this box vs 50 fp32, survey L91) — on the cached 0.5B the KV is 12,288 B/token (9% of bytes at 4K), nothing to win.

## Dead ends
- "Each layer" predicting through the tied head: every exit re-reads 26-28% of the model's bytes; a full-head drafter at layer k pays 1.2x only above the curve in claim 2 (at a=0.7 nothing deeper than layer 2 pays). The hierarchy collapses to ONE shallow split + one small head — DVI's measured 2.16x instance; the ladder is untested anywhere in the treasury.
- Deeper ladders / more draft tokens: 3 steps 2.76/1.91x vs 15 steps 3.66/1.70x (Eagle3 card); "8 rarely helps"; accepted length past the pp/tg crossover (~4-6 on 0.6B Q8, ~3.7 on 4B Q4) buys nothing on this iron.
- Parallel multi-token heads on one layer: Medusa 1.66x vs autoregressive one-layer drafters 2.16-2.18x, same harness (DVI Table 2).
- "Predicting" KV with a learned map saves no bytes — only an identical shared tensor does; a 1024x1024 map over 4096 cached positions is 8.6 GFLOP/layer/token vs ~0.94 GFLOP for all attention (ESTIMATE). Lossless half-sharing on the 0.6B caps at ~235 of ~1,110 MB/token at 4K (<= 1.27x), below the one-flag q4_0 KV (470 -> ~130 MB) that H3 has still not measured.
- Training a per-layer predictor on hit/miss (the flip/"prediction-based" instinct): DVI CE-only MAT 0.039, PG-only 0.035 (~0.34x). Calibrate on verifier logits first.
- Choosing exit/share layers by similarity or uniform interleave: IndexCache similarity-DP 49.8 < uniform 50.7; D1 global proxy +0.394 vs random +0.205 — pick layers by measured loss, per layer.
