## The idea
The paper (doc:draft-paper-v0, DRAFT v0.1, a MATH-class paper) makes claims of three kinds: proofs, measured numbers, and fit to the owner's rubric. Each kind can be checked by someone who did not write it. Three hypotheses, one per kind (the missing data folded into the numbers one), each closed by a verdict, turn "the draft looks fine" into a checked draft the owner can rewrite in his own words with confidence.
