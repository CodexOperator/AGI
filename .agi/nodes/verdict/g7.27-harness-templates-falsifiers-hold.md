  D.LOG [--model M] [--effort E] [--settings JSON] CARD`). At the tip it calls

  `templates/harness/claude-code.toml`.

  `Path(rotate.__file__).read_text()` and asserts neither

  absence of the named builders, not by measuring their bodies.
