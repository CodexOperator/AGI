### F1 — templates-only argv, no flag construction in rotate.py: GREEN

- `tests/test_harness_template.py:61` `::test_claude_builder_renders_frozen_argv`
  pins the PRODUCTION builder against the OLD hand-built argv as a LITERAL
  (`claude --remote-control N --permission-mode bypassPermissions --debug-file
  D.LOG [--model M] [--effort E] [--settings JSON] CARD`). At the tip it calls
  `rotate._build_harness_command("claude-code", ...)` — NOT the deleted
  `_build_claude_command`. It would catch a dropped/renamed flag in
  `templates/harness/claude-code.toml`.
- `tests/test_harness_template.py:102` `::test_copilot_builder_renders_frozen_argv`
  does the same for `rotate._build_harness_command("copilot-cli", ...)` and
  `templates/harness/copilot-cli.toml`.
- `tests/test_harness_template.py:84`
  `::test_no_named_harness_builders_in_rotate_source` reads
  `Path(rotate.__file__).read_text()` and asserts neither
  `_build_claude_command` nor `_build_copilot_command` appears in the SOURCE.
  THIS is the test that now pins "zero flag construction inside rotate.py" by
  absence of the named builders, not by measuring their bodies.
- `tests/test_harness_template.py:200` `::test_claude_production_path_reaches_render`
  monkeypatches `rotate.harness_template.render` with a sentinel and asserts
  `_build_harness_command(None, ...)` RETURNS the sentinel with
  `harness_id == "claude-code"` — i.e. the production claude path goes THROUGH
  the renderer, not an inline argv.
- `tests/test_harness_dispatch_shapes.py:166`
  `::test_adapter_production_path_reaches_render_with_the_dispatch_shape`
  is the same sentinel probe on the DISPATCH side (cc/copilot adapters), for
  both shipped harnesses.
- `tests/test_harness_dispatch_shapes.py:183`
  `::test_no_flag_construction_remains_in_either_adapter_body` strips
  docstrings/comments from each adapter's `build_command` body and asserts
  none of `--output-format --strict-mcp-config --add-dir --tools
  --allowedTools --disallowedTools --mcp-config --allow-all --remote -p`
  appears in live code.

Direct read of `rotate.py` corroborates: `_build_claude_command` and
`_build_copilot_command` are **GONE** at the tip —
`grep -n "_build_claude_command\|_build_copilot_command" extensions/agi/bin/rotate.py`
is EMPTY. The sole rotate argv seam is `rotate._build_harness_command`
(`rotate.py:1001`), whose body is a single
`return harness_template.render(harness or "claude-code", ...)`. The claim
"zero flag construction inside rotate.py" now holds by ABSENCE of the named
builders plus the single render seam, not by measuring hook bodies.

### F2 — a fourth harness adds a template without editing rotate.py: GREEN

- `tests/test_harness_template.py:147`
  `::test_synthetic_fourth_harness_renders_without_editing_rotate` writes a new
  `fake-harness.toml` into a tmp template dir, renders it, asserts the built
  argv, asserts it is in `available()`, and asserts
  `rotate._validate_harness(None, "fake-harness")[0] == 0` — accepted with no
  rotate allowlist edit.
- `tests/test_harness_template.py:176` `::test_build_harness_command_dispatches_on_template`
  asserts `rotate._build_harness_command("fake-harness", ...)` BUILDS the fake
  argv and `got[0] != "claude"` — no silent fallthrough.
- `rotate._known_harnesses()` (`rotate.py:950`) derives the buildable set from
  the templates on disk, per the code and its docstring.

### F3 — unexpressible bits stay behind a NAMED thin hook, no scripting escape hatch: GREEN

- `tests/test_harness_template.py:166`
  `::test_template_vocabulary_has_no_scripting_escape_hatch` writes a template
  with `script = "import os"` and asserts `harness_template.load("evil")`
  raises `HarnessTemplateError` — the vocabulary is declarative and closed, so
  a template can never become a program.
- The only remaining named thin hook/seam is `rotate._build_harness_command`
  (`rotate.py:1001`), a single `return harness_template.render(...)` that
  dispatches on the template. The two per-harness builders the old version of
  this verdict named (`rotate._build_claude_command` at `:896`,
  `rotate._build_copilot_command` at `:1014`) were DELETED by `goal:g7.27.1`
  and are absent at this tip. `harness_template.render` is the only argv
  producer.
