## Hypothesis

The cold director brief/custom-instruction surface names all five pane-facing
engine routes as a single contract vocabulary — **write · read · send ·
dispatch/workflow · rotate/spawn** — rather than burying the routes in
role-specific prose or presenting a sixth Grok-only route.

## Why this matters

`goal:g7.31.3.1` makes the five route names the contract. A cold seat should be
able to choose the correct named CLI seam without reconstructing a parallel
Grok-specific workflow.

## What proves it

- A cold/director brief source visibly lists the five route names in one place.
- The source identifies the underlying seams sufficiently to distinguish
  `read` (commands/viewport), `dispatch`/`workflow`, and `rotate`/`spawn`
  without inventing a new route.
- A deliberate route rename, if any, is represented as old→new vocabulary.

## What disproves it

The brief omits one of the five names, uses incompatible names without a rename
record, or introduces a sixth special route in the brief/dispatch/rotation
surface.

diagram, and `doc:director-grok-internals` §PROFILE. Both contain the compact
contract line `routes write·read·send·dispatch/workflow·rotate/spawn`; the
unified brief also says to prefer named CLIs / `write.py` over raw tools. This
is a static brief-surface check, not a live CLI execution (which belongs to
`goal:g7.31.3.2`).
