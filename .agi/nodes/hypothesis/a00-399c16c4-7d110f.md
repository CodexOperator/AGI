# hypothesis:a00-399c16c4-7d110f

## Hypothesis

**Claim.** The seat held across a child death is re-spawned through the SAME
filtered `child_env` the first spawn used, so a held seat on a
`credential: "none"` harness row never hands `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` to the
re-opened child — measured at the wire boundary (`kwargs["env"]` on
`subprocess.Popen`), not at an internal.

**Falsifier.** On tip, the HELD re-spawn's env differs from the first spawn's,
or carries the runtime key the first spawn correctly dropped.

## What the goal's named surface actually is

`goal:g7.31.1.2.1` was written against `HOLD_PANE=True` and `tmux_hold`
`start`/`reattach`/`_cmd`. Neither exists in this checkout (no `tmux` in
`dispatch.py`, no `adapters/tmux_hold.py`) — the prior kid's static probe was
right about the surface and wrong to leave the claim unmeasured. The real
durable named hold is the PERSISTENT seat (`goal:g7.28.1`):

- `dispatch._supervise_persistent` (dispatch.py ~L1518) holds the seat, and on
  death calls `reopen("ab")`;
- `reopen` IS the caller's `_open_round` closure (dispatch.py ~L2707), which
  holds the one rendered `spawn_args` and the one `spawn_env` and passes
  `env=spawn_env` into `subprocess.Popen` (~L2716);
- `spawn_env` is built ONCE at ~L2561 from
  `adapter.child_env(harness=…, base=scrubbed_env(), tier=…)`, and the
  credential drop lives inside `child_env`
  (`adapters.drop_unneeded_credential`) rather than in a per-call-site pop.

So the goal's target end-state already holds in production on this tip. What
was genuinely missing was any TEST naming that seam: `test_dispatch_persistent.py`
proved the restart reuses the identical argv but never looked at `env`, and
`test_credential_none_spawn.py` covered the live spawn and the adapter
`restart` seam — not the held seat.
