# hypothesis:a00-44b059ee-5c4568

## Hypothesis

**Claim.** A *live* `workflow.py run` (not `--dry-run`) on the no-credential
harness `pi-local` executes the stage through the named workflow CLI and
spawns the harness binary directly — no parallel script, and no second
router (`goal:g1.14`). The dry-run banner's phrase `via dispatch.py kids`
therefore misnames the live mechanism on this path.

**Prove it if:** a live run of `extensions/agi/bin/workflow.py` on
`--harness pi-local` completes (or is captured at the exec) and the exact
spawned argv contains the harness `bin` and `-p --provider … --model …
--thinking … <prompt>` with no `dispatch.py` in it, and no other writer or
dispatcher appears in the transcript.

**Disprove it if:** the live path shells out to `dispatch.py` (or any
script that is not the named CLI); the stage never spawns; or a foreign
dispatcher appears in the transcript.

This round settles exactly the one conjunct the previous kid left
resolve-only, on the live path:
`experiment:live-workflow-pi-local-spawn-argv-a00-44b059ee`.

## What the live run found

Two live runs through the real `workflow.py` (details and raw bytes on the
experiment node):

1. **shim bin, `--harness pi-local`** — full live path, `rc 0`. The argv
   handed to the harness bin was
   `["-p", "--provider", "openrouter", "--model", "Qwen3.5-9B-Q4_K_M", "--thinking", "medium", <prompt>]`.
2. **real pi bin, strace'd** — `execve("/home/ubuntu/.npm-global/bin/pi",
   ["/home/ubuntu/.npm-global/bin/pi", "-p", "--provider", "openrouter", …]) = 0`.

`grep dispatch.py` over the execve log: **0 lines**. So:

- **Target falsifier GREEN.** The action goes through the named CLI
  (`workflow.py`), not a parallel script. `workflow.py` is the **router**;
  it execs the pi binary itself (`_run_stage_pi`, `workflow.py:1755`, argv at
  `:1793`). `dispatch.py` is the **spawner of agent kids** for graph rounds
  and is not on this path.
- The last kid's conjunct-3 reading — "workflow resolves to dispatch.py
  kids" — is **false**, but it was a reading of a `--dry-run` LABEL
  (`workflow.py:2157`), not the live mechanism. Already recorded at
  `goal:g14:177`; this round adds a live execve.
