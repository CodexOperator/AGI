# hypothesis:a00-0211b6bc-cf8751

## Hypothesis

The cold-seat instruction surface can expose one canonical, compact route
list — **write**, **read**, **send**, **dispatch | workflow**, and **rotate |
spawn** — without introducing a sixth grok-specific route or a second
workflow router. The list should name the engine seams (`write.py`,
`commands.py`/viewport, `send.py`, `workflow.py` behind `dispatch.py`, and
`rotate.py`) so a new seat can act through the existing panes rather than
rediscover raw-tool variants.

**Proves it:** a cold-seat brief or custom-instruction artifact is found that
contains all five contract names, maps each to the engine seam, and explicitly
says the workflow route is the single sanctioned router. A grep/read of that
artifact is sufficient evidence.

**Disproves it:** the surface omits or renames one of the five without an
old-to-new migration note, routes workflow through a parallel invoker, or
introduces a harness-specific sixth route in the dispatch/rotate seam.
