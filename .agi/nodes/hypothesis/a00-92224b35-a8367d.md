## Hypothesis

A cold-seat custom-instruction surface can satisfy `goal:g7.31.3.1` with one compact route table that gives the exact five contract names, their engine seams, and their preferred invocation:

| pane-facing route | engine seam |
|---|---|
| `write` | `write.py` |
| `read` | `commands.py` / viewport `see`/`read` |
| `send` | `send.py` |
| `dispatch | workflow` | `dispatch.py` + `workflow.py` |
| `rotate | spawn` | `rotate.py` |
