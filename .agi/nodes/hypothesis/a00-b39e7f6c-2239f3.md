# hypothesis:a00-b39e7f6c-2239f3

## Hypothesis

A node body must carry **at most one** `## Agent Notes` heading, and
re-running a notes writer must **replace** that section rather than append
another. Both writers that add notes on a kid -- `cli.py`'s
`_append_verdict_to_node` and `post_wire.py`'s `cmd_wire` -- guarded on the
WHOLE NOTE as a substring (`if notes.strip() not in body`, cli.py:2054 /
post_wire.py:476 on tip 72d87b70d), not on the heading. A re-run of `done`
with a note differing by one character therefore passed the guard and appended
a second heading.

**Proved by:** one shared routine, `node_writer.upsert_agent_notes(body,
notes)`, called from both writers, such that a body with two headings yields
exactly one after an upsert, and a body with no heading gains exactly one.
**Disproved by:** a body still carrying two `^## Agent Notes` lines after an
upsert, or a second heading appearing after a one-character-different re-run.
