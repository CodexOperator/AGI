## Hypothesis

call site, not only in scratch: after landing
`extensions/agi/bin/adapters/tmux_hold.py` on tip, declaring `HOLD_PANE` +

`dispatch.py`, and routing `grok_bot_adapter.restart` through
`tmux_hold.reattach`, a real `kill -9` of the seat's pane process followed by
the real `grok_bot_adapter.restart` re-enters the SAME tmux pane.

## Testable claim

Against a real tmux server: spawn the seat through the first-spawn seam
dispatch uses, `kill -9` its `#{pane_pid}`, call
`grok_bot_adapter.restart(...)`. The window name and `#{pane_id}` are
identical BEFORE/MID/AFTER; only `#{pane_pid}` churns; the record the adapter
writes carries `created=False`.

## Falsifier / disproof

Any capture where `restart` fabricates a second pane (`#{pane_id}` changes,
or a second seat window appears), or where the pane is gone at process death.

identity, which is what the goal asks for.
