detects a message transport on the crons node's `services:` table *by table

inline loop flag is flagged — while the two benign live services
(`heal.py watch`, `rotate.py alarms`) and the allowed one-shot `send.py wake`
tick are not.

version: that version required a transport line to also carry `--poll`/

`service agi-message-router exec_start .../send.py router restart on-failure`
— the natural way a `services:` row is declared. The old scan's negative
control fired only because its synthetic row *also* carried `--poll-s 30
--daemon`.

service-row the parent showed was missed — transport, `restart on-failure`,
no loop flag. (b) It still fires on a `send.py recv --box-local` /
`send.py serve-room` service row. (c) It returns `[]` for the two live benign
services and for the live one-shot `send.py wake --all-local` tick. (d) The
live-surface test still passes against the live node.

**What disproves it.** The corrected scanner returns `[]` on a bare
service-row transport (guard still vacuous), OR it flags a benign live service
or the one-shot tick (guard too broad).
