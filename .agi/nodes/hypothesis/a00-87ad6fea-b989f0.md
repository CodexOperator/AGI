`send.py serve-room` service row. (c) It returns `[]` for the two live benign
services and for the live one-shot `send.py wake --all-local` tick. (d) The
live-surface test still passes against the live node.

service-row transport (guard still vacuous), OR it flags a benign live service
or the one-shot tick (guard too broad).
