# hypothesis:a00-a6befda6-499715

## Hypothesis

**A single adapter module can carry an OPTIONAL pane surface behind one
generic, harness-neutral interface, and calling it without a held pane fails
closed with a named error.**

Testable claim: `grok_bot_adapter` exposes `pane_attach / pane_send /
pane_read` while every pane-less adapter (`pi`, …) omits them; the generic
`adapters.has_pane_methods()` is the ONE feature-detection seam; and with no
`pane` cell on the harness row each method raises `PaneNotHeld` promptly
instead of hanging or silently no-op'ing.

Disproved if: a second `grok*_adapter.py` were needed; if the required g7.25
surface changed; if the pane methods owned hold semantics rather than wrapping
one; or if a no-pane call hung / raised an anonymous error.

## What was built (goal:g7.32.3 falsifier, all three)

1. `adapters/__init__.py` — `PANE_METHODS = ("pane_attach", "pane_send",
   "pane_read")` plus `has_pane_methods(module)`, generic and harness-neutral.
2. `adapters/grok_bot_adapter.py` — the three optional methods. They read the
   held pane NAME from the harness row's `pane` cell and route every tmux call
   through ONE injectable `_tmux(args)` seam. No held pane ⇒ `PaneNotHeld`
   (`RuntimeError` subclass), raised before any call. They never create, own or
   reap a pane — that stays `goal:g7.31.1.2`.
3. `pi_adapter.py` and the other adapters are untouched, so absence of the
   methods IS the non-pane path.
