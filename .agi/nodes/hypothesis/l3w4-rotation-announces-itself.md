WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE BUILD, and it is a small addition to the same helper, not a second mechanism. A rotation announcement is one instance of a general shape: A STATE-CHANGING ORDER BROADCAST TO SEATS. Rotation is the cheapest one to build against because it is scheduled, structured and already has a durable record. But the announcement payload must carry a MONOTONIC SEQUENCE NUMBER and the seat-visible read path must let any reader ask, cheaply and without a round trip, "is the order I am holding still the current one?" A timestamp is not sufficient and today proved why: every party had timestamps, nobody compared them, and the two orders were six hours apart. A reader that must NOTICE something will eventually not notice it; a reader that must CHECK a counter cannot silently hold a superseded order. Concretely: stamp each announcement with a sequence taken from a durable per-project counter, record it alongside the rotation record, and expose the current value so a seat can compare in one read. Anything that reaches a seat and changes what it is allowed to do should be able to ride the same field later — the stop and the go included — but DO NOT build a general order bus in this brief; build the sequence into the announcement and leave the generalisation to a follow-up.
