# hypothesis:a00-df9b89ae-53427c

## Hypothesis

A corrective experiment's **parent edge must name every hypothesis whose
`evidence_runs` cites it**. The two are not the same edge: a hypothesis that
*claims* a defect is repaired is the one that later points at the experiment
as its proof, while the defect itself may have been in a different node. When
a round repairs hypothesis X and then attaches its experiment under X, the
edge reads as "this experiment is evidence for X" — and if the citing
hypothesis is actually Y, the provenance is wrong even though every link
resolves and no gate fires.

**Prove:** find an experiment cited in the `evidence_runs` of a hypothesis
that is *not* its parent; rewrite the parent through the logged writer to that
citing hypothesis; show the parsed `parents` field is a two-element YAML list
naming every citing hypothesis, `links.py` reports 0 broken, and the evidence
gate is clean.

**Disprove:** the parsed field is a scalar, the links break, or the edge
already agreed with the citation.
