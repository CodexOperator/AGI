# hypothesis:a00-78734604-c5bdb9 — the `loop` rotation path must refuse profile drift

## Hypothesis (testable claim)

`cmd_loop` (`extensions/agi/bin/rotate.py:3006`) is a seat-rotation path —
the super-ralph primitive that meters, rotates when due, and spawns the
successor. It calls `_check_branch_guard(root)` at `:3019` and
`spawn_window(...)` at `:3050`, but **never** `_check_profile_drift`.
Therefore a deliberate desync of a `profile_ref`-linked artifact does not
block a `loop` rotation, and `goal:g7.31.5.3`'s falsifier ("detected …
before the next seat rotation") is not met on this path.

**Claim.** Wiring `_check_profile_drift(root)` into `cmd_loop` immediately
after the branch guard — before the meter check and before `spawn_window` —
makes `cmd_loop` refuse a drifted linked artifact by name with exit 1, and
never reach `spawn_window`.

**Proved by:** a behavioural probe that fails when `cmd_loop` omits the
guard (build a scratch repo, mutate a linked artifact, monkeypatch
`rotate.spawn_window` with a recorder, call `cmd_loop`, assert the refusal
message AND `recorder == []`). **Disproved by:** the guard fires after
`spawn_window` is reached, or the refusal is not by name.

## `cmd_spawn` — decided, not left open

`cmd_spawn` (`rotate.py:1963`) also skips `_check_profile_drift`. **Decision:
(b) — leave it unguarded, and here is the mechanism.**

1. `cmd_spawn` is the **first-seating / manual-launch** actuator (used by
   `rotate.py spawn --seat` and by the seats-launch path), not a rotation of
   a running seat. The residue's falsifier is specifically "before the next
   seat **rotation**"; both rotation primitives — `cmd_rotate_self` and now
   `cmd_loop` — are gated.
2. It is the **recovery** actuator: refusing `rotate.py spawn` while the graph
   is desynced would refuse the very spawn a human or healer needs to seat a
   recovering post. A guard that blocks recovery is not a guard.
3. `heal.py:2663` calls `_rotate.spawn_window(...)` **directly**, bypassing
   `cmd_spawn` entirely. Wiring `cmd_spawn` would therefore not gate healing
   anyway — it would only half-close a path and imply a coverage that does
   not exist. The one launch primitive (`spawn_window`) is deliberately
   guard-free; the two *rotation* callers carry the guard.

If a later round decides the falsifier covers first seating too, the fix
belongs at `spawn_window` (one site, all three callers) — not at `cmd_spawn`
alone.
