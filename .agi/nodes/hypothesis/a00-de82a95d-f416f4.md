# hypothesis:a00-de82a95d-f416f4

## Hypothesis

A unified `send(src_harness, dst_harness, *, seat, text, pane_seam=None,
 nudge_dir=None, send_py=None)` entrypoint in `extensions/agi/bin/messaging.py`
makes the native-vs-nudge transport choice **structural**: `route()` decides,
same-harness cannot reach `send.py`, and cross-harness **refuses by name**
when `nudge_dir`/`send_py` are absent instead of silently falling back to the
native pane.

## Testable claim

1. `send("grok-bot","grok-bot",…)` returns `path="native"`, `send_py=None`,
   and shells nothing (zero `send.py` invocations).
2. `send("grok-bot","claude-code",…,nudge_dir=…,send_py=…)` returns
   `path="nudge"` with BOTH `nudge_artifact` and `send_py` in ONE trace.
3. `send("grok-bot","pi",…)` with no `send_py`/`nudge_dir` raises `ValueError`
   naming the missing argument(s), and never touches the native seam.

## What would disprove it

A same-harness `send()` that shells `send.py`; a cross `send()` that returns
two traces or no artifact; a transport-less cross `send()` that returns a
`native` trace instead of refusing.
