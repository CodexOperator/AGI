## Why `--owns` and not `--node-id`

`--node-id` names the node this round created but does not add foreign paths to
`own_paths`, so the round-scope rule refuses the two corrected files as FOREIGN
and they stay uncommitted -- the exact failure the DH.97 residue round hit.
`--owns` puts each named id in `own_paths`, and `cli.py done` then commits them
with this round. That is the entire content of this hypothesis.
