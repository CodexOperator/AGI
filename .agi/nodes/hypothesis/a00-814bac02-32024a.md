# hypothesis:a00-814bac02-32024a

## Hypothesis

On the DT.102 tip (`f655a6714`), a `grok-bot` seat can hold a durable NAMED
tmux pane: killing the seat process leaves its immutable `#{pane_id}` alive,
and `restart` re-enters that SAME pane (`tmux respawn-pane -k` on the
pane_id) rather than spawning a fresh anonymous one. The hold is an opt-in
seam declared in the adapter (`HOLD_PANE` / `hold_harness`) and applied in
`restart` too, so an older record with no `tmux` cell still re-enters its
pane; the dispatch restart call site is generic (no `grok` literal).

**Proved by:** real tmux — same `#{pane_id}` before/after `kill -9`, plus a
renamed-seat probe and a pane-gone probe stamping `created=true`.
**Disproved by:** a changed `#{pane_id}` after restart, a fresh pane when the
old one lived, or another seat name entering the same pane.

## Evidence

Child experiment `experiment:durable-named-tmux-pane-hold-live-reproof`
carries the raw live output, the negative probes and the wire probe. The
build is real tmux, not a fixtures-only monkeypatch.
