- **`workflow.py status <key>` can return exit 1 "no runs match key" for a mur/research-review that is genuinely still in flight and will register once stages start completing.** Ground truth while running: `systemctl --user status <unit>` + its journal (stage-by-stage `[~]`/`[✓]` lines), or `.agi/sessions/workflows/runs/<key>/*.json` per-stage files directly once they exist.
