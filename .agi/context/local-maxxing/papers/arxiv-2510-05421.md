## Summary (measured lines)

- Self-speculative decoding on ONE backbone: the model is split at layer k; a LoRA-parameterised draft head on the shallow state h_k proposes a block of k_spec tokens, the frozen deep path (layers k..L) plus the original LM head verifies with longest-prefix greedy matching, so it is lossless. Experiment split: "layer 2 is the drafter, while layers 3 through 32 are the verifier", k_spec = 4, backbone Vicuna-7B (Spec-Bench mandated).
- The new part is "Improve": every verify step logs one tuple per drafted position up to and including the first reject (r=1 accepted, r=0 first reject; positions past the first reject are dropped as counterfactual) into a replay buffer and the draft-head LoRA is updated online. Objective is a KL→RL schedule: KL to the verifier logits (online distillation) during warmup, then a ramp adds reward-masked cross-entropy on accepted positions and a REINFORCE term with an EMA baseline; backbone and verifier never change.
- Training budget: "2000 steps over 2000 prompts, such that the model sees each prompt only once" (ShareGPT). Table 1: DVI 2,000 prompt exposures vs Medusa 120,000 ("~60x more"), Kangaroo 1,200,000 ("~600x more"), EAGLE 2,400,000 ("~1,200x more"); optimiser steps 2,000 vs ~945 / ~4,700 / ~300,000.
- Result (Table 2, Spec-Bench, single H100, greedy): DVI average wall-time speedup 2.16x vs EAGLE-2 2.18x, EAGLE-1 2.05x, Hydra 1.96x, Medusa 1.66x, PLD 1.62x, SpS 1.48x. DVI mean accepted tokens (MAT) 3.04-3.61 per verify step vs EAGLE-2 3.22-4.75 — lower MAT, same speedup: "DVI converts agreement into throughput more efficiently" because a 2-layer drafter with depth-4 single-sequence proposals has no tree-building cost.
- Ablations (Table 3, final Spec-Bench): KL-only MAT 1.933 / 1.435x; PG-only MAT 0.035 / 0.341x; CE-only MAT 0.039 / 0.335x — i.e. reward-only ("prediction hit/miss") training is "~3x slower than a baseline model"; KL-only batch acceptance "begins to level out around a 80% batch acceptance rate". The full schedule is what reaches 2.16x.
- Caveat the authors state: absolute speedups for ALL methods in their shared harness are lower than the respective papers report (repeated on an A40 with the same result); only the relative ordering is trusted.

## Relevance to local-maxxing

Bytes-per-token ledger (the swarm box is bandwidth-bound, so weight bytes touched per committed token IS the speed): AR reads the whole model once per token. DVI-style self-speculation at split 2 / k_spec 4 reads the draft path k_spec times and the deep path once per block, and commits MAT tokens per block. The paper's 7B never noticed the LM head (Vicuna's 32k×4096 head is ~2% of weights), but on the town's tied-embedding Qwen small models the head is 26-28% of all weight bytes (numbers above), so a drafter that runs the frozen full-vocab head per draft token costs 4 × (0.053 + 0.261) = 1.26 full-pass-equivalents BEFORE the 0.95 verify pass: 2.20 passes per block, /MAT 3 = 0.73 of AR bytes — a 1.4x ceiling, roughly what a KL-only drafter delivered in the paper (1.435x). Shrink the draft head to a rank-64 factored projection (1024×64 + 64×151,936 ≈ 9.8M = 1.6%) and the block costs 4 × 0.069 + 0.947 = 1.22 passes, /MAT 3 = 0.41 of AR bytes — a 2.4x ceiling on a 23 GB no-GPU box that cannot hold a second draft model. That is the one town-specific lever this paper exposes.

Looped transformer: a depth-recurrent model has the DVI split built in — draft at 1 loop, verify at N loops, same weights, no LoRA drafter needed except the head; the accept/reject stream is the free online supervision for a 1-loop exit head. Note for the ledger: a ~16 MB Q8 loop body does not fit the A1's cache, so a looped model still pays body-bytes × N per token; block-verifying k_spec tokens at once amortises that, which is what makes the two ideas layer rather than merely coexist.

Flip/SNN + owner's "prediction-based rather than probability-based" hunch: DVI is a measured warning. Training the drafter only on binary hit/miss (CE-only MAT 0.039, PG-only 0.035, both ~0.34x, i.e. 3x slower than no speculation) collapses; the dense probability-matching KL term is what bootstraps to ~80% acceptance and the binary signal only helps as a late on-policy correction. A flip-coded predictor trained on impulses alone should be expected to hit the same wall unless it is first calibrated against a probability target. Owner's "each layer predicts 2-3 tokens" hunch: DVI is its 1-level instance (layer 2 predicts 4, deep path verifies, 3.0-3.6 accepted on a 7B); the paper does not test a multi-level ladder. Cluster: verify on local-town, draft on CPU is the natural split of the DVI geometry, but WAN latency (unmeasured) is the falsifier for any cross-box scheme; the seed below stays on one box.

## Idea seed

- slug: `lm-self-spec-small-draft-head`
- title: Self-speculation on a 0.6B needs a small draft head, not just a shallow drafter
- lever: On a tied-embedding Qwen3-0.6B the LM head is 26.1% of weight bytes, so a layer-2 drafter that reuses it costs 1.26 full passes per 4-token block; replacing it with a rank-64 factored draft head (1.6%) drops block cost from 2.20 to 1.22 full-pass-equivalents, i.e. from 0.73 to 0.41 of AR weight bytes per committed token at MAT 3 (2.4x bandwidth ceiling vs 1.4x).
- buys: Lossless ~2x decode on the swarm box (Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 from 34-45 tok/s toward 70-90) with zero second model in the 23 GB and a drafter head trained online from accept/reject bits in a few thousand prompts on local-town, then shipped as a tiny separate-GGUF drafter for llama.cpp's speculative path.
- first_falsifier: Held-out MAT of the rank-64 layer-2 draft head at k_spec = 4 after 2,000-prompt KL-only online distillation is below 2.0 (paper's KL-only on a 7B reached 1.933 with the full head), which would cap the ledger at 1.22/2 = 0.61 of AR bytes and make the whole scheme worth less than the Q4 quant the town already has.
- cheapest_test_on_our_iron: local-town (gpu-8g, torch venv): Qwen3-0.6B fp16 frozen, rank-64 draft head on layer-2 output, KL-only online distillation over 2,000 ShareGPT prompts with the paper's log-up-to-first-reject buffer, report batch-acceptance curve and held-out MAT at k_spec = 4, ~1-2 h wall-clock, $0.

## Critique (adversarial, 2026-09-16)

Re-fetched: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05421 HTTP 200 162,270 bytes; https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05421 HTTP 200 41,960 bytes (`citation_date 2025/10/06`, cs.LG, CC BY 4.0); side configs https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B/raw/main/config.json HTTP 200 and https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B/raw/main/config.json HTTP 200. Every paper number in the digest (Table 1 budgets, Table 2 MAT/speedups for all 7 methods x 6 tasks, Table 3 ablations, split/k_spec/H100/A40 statements, "~80% batch acceptance", "~3x slower") was found verbatim in the fetched text. The reader's Qwen arithmetic reproduces exactly (Qwen3-0.6B: 15.7M/layer, 155.6M tied head = 26.1% of 596M, two layers 5.3%; block 2.202 -> /3 = 0.734; rank-64 head 9.79M = 1.64%, block 1.224 -> /3 = 0.408; Qwen2.5-0.5B: 14.9M/layer, 136.1M = 27.6% of 494M, two layers 6.0%). The errors are in what the digest attributes to the source and in what the seed extrapolates.

1. **"a layer-2 drafter reusing the full head" / "runs the frozen full-vocab head" (Relevance, seed lever).** The source never says the draft projection is the LM head. Sec 3.1: "$W^{(S)}$ is a frozen base projection at the draft path output, and $(A_s,B_s)$ are trainable LoRA modules"; the verifier head is a separate symbol $W^{(V)}$. Whether $W^{(S)}=W^{(V)}$, and how $W^{(S)}$ is initialised, is unstated. Byte-wise the reader's assumption is still right — $W^{(S)}$ maps $h_k$ to a softmax over the vocabulary, so it is vocab x hidden regardless — but "reuses the LM head" is the reader's, not the paper's.
2. **"rank-64" (seed lever, falsifier, test).** No LoRA rank appears anywhere in the source; the only rank-related phrase is "stabilize gradients in the low-rank subspace" (Sec 3.4). More importantly, the paper's mechanism is *full-size frozen projection + LoRA delta* — the full $W^{(S)}$ is still read on every draft step. A **factored rank-64 replacement head** (no full projection) is the reader's design, not a mechanism the source demonstrates. The seed's lever therefore rests on an untested drafter, and comparing its hoped-for MAT against Table 3's KL-only 1.933 (a full-vocab head + LoRA on a 7B) is not a like-for-like anchor.
3. **"draft path read k_spec times per block" (Relevance ledger).** Source Sec 3.2 says the opposite, literally: "Drafting a $k_{\text{spec}}$-token block requires one shallow forward; if $m$ tokens are accepted, the deep computation is amortized over those $m$ outputs in a single verification pass." Autoregressive drafting with a single next-token classifier does need $k_{\text{spec}}$ sequential shallow passes, so the reader's 4x is the physically sensible reading — but it contradicts the paper's sentence and must be labelled as the reader's model. The ledger's 2.20 / 1.22 / 0.73 / 0.41 / "1.4x ceiling" / "2.4x ceiling" are all reader arithmetic on that assumption; none is in the source.
4. **"roughly what a KL-only drafter delivered in the paper (1.435x)" ~ "a 1.4x ceiling" (Relevance).** Spurious equivalence. Table 3's 1.435x is Spec-Bench wall-time on an H100 with Vicuna-7B; the 1.4x is a bytes-per-token ceiling for a 0.6B on a CPU. Different quantity, different hardware, different model; the coincidence carries no evidence.
5. **"Vicuna's 32k x 4096 head is ~2% of weights" (Relevance).** Not in the source (the paper never mentions head size, tied embeddings, bytes or bandwidth — grep for `bandwidth|bytes|tied|embedding|LM head` returns only the EAGLE related-work line). Reader arithmetic without a fetched Vicuna config; plausible, unsupported.
6. **"a ~16 MB Q8 loop body does not fit the A1's cache" (Relevance, looped-transformer paragraph).** Neither number is from the source or from any fetch listed in Provenance.
7. **"2-layer drafter" (Summary, ledger).** Source: "layers indexed $0{:}L$", "shallow layers up to index $k{=}2$", and "layer 2 is the drafter, while layers 3 through 32 are the verifier". With 32 Vicuna layers and verifier = 3..32 (30 layers), two drafting layers is the consistent reading, but the 0-indexed notation admits three; the ledger's "0.053" depends on the choice. Flag, not a refutation.
8. **Seed `buys`: "Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 from 34-45 tok/s toward 70-90".** Extrapolated from the reader's ceiling; the source gives no CPU, no small-model and no bandwidth-bound result. Unsupported.
9. **Seed `buys`: "shipped as a tiny separate-GGUF drafter for llama.cpp's speculative path".** llama.cpp's speculative path takes a standalone draft *model*; a 2-layer Qwen3 with a factored rank-64 output head is not a stock GGUF architecture, so shipping it means either engine changes or a full tied output head (155.6M params) — the very bytes the lever removes. Also, as a separate draft model, the drafter no longer shares layers 0-2 with the target, so the verify pass reads all 28 layers (1.0) rather than 26+head (0.947): block 4 x 0.069 + 1.0 = 1.28, /3 = 0.43 — small change, but the "self" in self-speculation is lost on that engine. Not a source error; a feasibility error in the seed.
10. **Seed `buys` vs `cheapest_test`: internal contradiction.** `buys` says the head is "trained online from accept/reject bits"; the test is "KL-only online distillation", which uses verifier logits and no accept/reject bit (Sec 4.3: KL-only "equivalent to online distillation"). The source's own Table 3 says bits alone collapse (0.035/0.039 MAT). Say KL-warmup then bits, or drop the bits claim.
11. **Seed `cheapest_test`: "~1-2 h wall-clock".** The source reports no training wall-clock at all; the only "wall-clock" in the text is the schedule variable $t$ (Sec 3.4). Reader estimate, unsupported.
12. **"lossless" (Summary, seed).** True in the source only under greedy decoding: "we employ greedy decoding" and "verification preserves the target sampler's output" (Sec 3.1); App A: "greedy decoding (temperature 0) for verification". The town's sampled decodes would need the rejection-sampling verifier the paper does not use.
13. **Missing falsifier the town can run for free.** The whole ledger assumes the 5-token verify pass (k_spec + bonus) costs ~one weight read on the swarm box, i.e. that a batch-5 forward of Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 on the arm-cloud is still bandwidth-bound rather than compute-bound. Nothing in the source (H100) speaks to this, and it is a 5-minute `llama-bench` measurement that precedes any local-town training run.

Verdict: paper facts grounded (5/5); mechanism attribution and seed extrapolation over-reach (items 1-3, 8-11). Keep the digest; the seed survives only as "test whether a small factored draft head on a shallow split reaches MAT >= 2 at all", with the swarm-box batch-cost measurement as the first gate.
