## Finding

There is **nothing off-the-shelf that plugs directly into the two TypeSafe keys as a complete computer-use agent**. Cua is the strongest substrate because its prior measured `cb interact` run is headless, Docker-free, API-key-free for the simulated oracle, and emits action-plus-observation trajectory records. It still needs a model-driven policy for real prose intent. The practical split is: TypeSafe/Jev supplies bounded typed ranking or validation; a provider-neutral adapter (or local OpenAI-compatible model) supplies general computer-use reasoning; the action registry remains the single propose/validate/execute surface for both.

## Limits / falsifier check

The survey has four rows, but `openjev` is explicitly demoted because the reachable public landing did not yield a stable dependency contract. Therefore the hypothesis passes only on the three concrete rows (`cua`, Browser Use, LangGraph), not on the owner-named openjev name. If those public README claims cannot be re-fetched or differ materially, the row is a demote and the hypothesis should be reported as inconclusive rather than padded.
